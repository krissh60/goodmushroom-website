OEM / white-label
For private label & OEM teams

What private-label brands
get from Good Mushroom.

Custom mushroom blends — your formulation

Send a blend specification or product brief. Species, ratio, intended format, packaging, testing and finished-product claims are assessed with the relevant supplier or partner route. A custom blend is quoted only when the required inputs, documentation and commercial scope can be confirmed.

COA and lot evidence for the selected route

COA ownership, lot reference, traceability and permitted brand presentation are confirmed for the agreed route. Partner-lab evidence is shared where commissioned; Good Mushroom does not represent a supplier or laboratory credential as its own without the relevant document.

Capsule-ready, gummy-ready, stick-pack-ready

Capsules, gummies, stick packs and other finished formats have different flow, stability, sensory and packaging needs. Compatibility is checked against the selected ingredient, partner capability and buyer specification; no format is treated as universally ready without that review.

Packaging — bulk or your retail format

Bulk or retail packaging can be scoped around the product, stability needs, artwork, destination and supplier route. Packaging material, pack size, traceability presentation and any finished-pack service are confirmed in writing; Good Mushroom does not describe a partner facility as its own.

MOQ, lead time and repeat supply

Private-label MOQ, formulation scope, packaging, sample approval, testing and lead time are confirmed after the species, formula, artwork and destination are reviewed. Repeat-supply terms are agreed case by case and are not treated as a universal production or pricing promise.
