What pharmaceutical buyers
get from Good Mushroom.

Documented sourcing protocol

Pharmaceutical buyers need a clear product specification, evidence boundaries and a documented commercial route. We review the supplier information, product format, available quality evidence, packaging and destination requirements against the buyer brief. Any evidence or documentation is confirmed for the selected route rather than presented as a universal record set.

Active-ingredient testing by agreed specification

Specify the analyte, method, acceptance criteria and destination requirements in the buyer brief. Any active or safety result must identify the relevant lot and method; testing is commissioned or reviewed only where the selected product route supports it.

Excipient and raw-material formats

Possible routes include dried material, powder and extract formats, subject to the species, process route and buyer specification. Format, mesh, assay, packaging and documentation are confirmed before quote.

Ayurvedic herb supply — route-specific documentation

For pharmaceutical buyers sourcing Ayurvedic raw materials — ashwagandha, tulsi, brahmi, shatavari and moringa — the product, assay, supplier documents and destination requirements are reviewed before quoting. Registration or Ministry-of-AYUSH documentation is not represented as held without current evidence; partner-lab testing is commissioned only where required by the product route.

Documentation that pharmaceutical buyers actually need

Depending on the product route, we can discuss document requests such as a method of analysis, relevant stability or safety evidence, allergen statement, country-of-origin certificate and other buyer-specific records. Certification and regulatory documents are checked for the selected supplier and destination; additional time or cost is confirmed in writing.

Single point of responsibility

Pharmaceutical buyers need a clear counterparty and an agreed review process. We coordinate the trading route and review available quality evidence with the buyer; any replacement or commercial remedy is agreed in writing for the specific order.
