How buying from us works

Plain-English
buyer guide.

Everything a sourcing manager needs to know before sending a brief. If you'd rather skip ahead, the buyer enquiry form takes about 90 seconds.

Who buys from Good Mushroom Pvt Ltd

Buyer enquiries commonly cover supplement and nutraceutical ingredients, functional food and beverage formats, culinary mushrooms, private-label/OEM work and Ayurvedic herb routes. The product specification, destination and evidence requirements determine the appropriate route; this page does not claim a completed purchase history or repeat-buyer volume.

Minimum order quantity (MOQ) by product

MOQ depends on species, format, supplier route, testing, packaging and destination. Ask for the current route-specific MOQ in the quote; do not use a catalogue number as a universal commitment. Mixed-SKU or mixed-pallet feasibility is also confirmed case by case.

Pricing model — how we quote

Quotes can be prepared on an FOB or destination basis when the product route is confirmed (Mumbai, Delhi or Bangalore depending on product) and CIF landed pricing to your destination port. Pricing validity, recurring supply and any contract terms are confirmed in the written quote. The written quote separates sourcing, quality review, logistics and any applicable commercial charges. You pay the farm-gate price plus our quality review + partner handling + logistics — disclosed line by line on request for buyers on annual contracts.

Lab testing — what can be commissioned by route

Where testing is commissioned for the product route, an accredited partner-lab Certificate of Analysis can be shared. The COA reports actives by HPLC (cordycepin, hericenones, triterpenes, β-glucan, withanolides — whichever apply to your product), heavy metals by ICP-MS (lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury), full microbiological panel (TPC, yeast and mould, E. coli, salmonella), pesticide residue screening, and mycotoxin testing where applicable. We can share recent batch COAs at quote stage so your QA team can verify against your in-house spec before you commit to a paid sample.

Sample process and lead times

Sample size, sample charge, credit terms, dispatch timing and commercial lead time are confirmed for the selected route. Transport mode and destination documents are also reviewed before a shipment commitment is made.

Export documentation we provide

Documentation is confirmed for the specific product and destination. Where applicable and available, the route may include a partner-lab COA, commercial invoice, packing list and freight documents. Certification or registration status is checked for the selected route and is not represented as held without current evidence.

Certifications held by Good Mushroom Pvt Ltd

Good Mushroom is a sourcing and trading company. Testing and destination documents are commissioned or requested through the relevant supplier/partner route when required; any company registration, certification or phytosanitary status is confirmed before it is included in a quote.
