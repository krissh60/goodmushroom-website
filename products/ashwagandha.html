supply, explained.

Bulk ashwagandha root and extract supply for Ayurveda, supplement and nutraceutical brands — formats, withanolides, certifications.

Bulk ashwagandha root — supply formats

We can discuss Withania somnifera root, powder and extract routes through the relevant supplier network. The grade, moisture, mesh, extraction method, assay, MOQ, packaging and destination documents are confirmed against the current buyer specification.

Root, powder or extract: what belongs in your brief

  • Whole or cut root: specify botanical identity, root-only requirement, cut size, cleanliness and moisture acceptance criteria.
  • Root powder: add particle size or mesh, any processing restrictions, intended application and packaging requirements.
  • Extract: state the plant part, extraction requirements, carrier restrictions and withanolide target with the required test method. A generic extract is not automatically equivalent to a branded extract.

For a sample review, include the format, quantity needed, delivery location and tests you plan to run. Availability, cost, courier terms and whether a sample represents an offered lot are agreed before dispatch.

Withanolide testing by product specification

Buyers who require a withanolide result should specify the analyte, method and acceptance criteria. We discuss testing against that brief; any assay value must come from the relevant lot report or a separately commissioned test.

Ashwagandha buyers — who orders this ingredient

Potential use cases to discuss include Ayurveda and traditional-formulation routes, nutraceutical powders and extracts, and private-label ingredient programmes. The relevant assay, regulatory documentation and intended claims must be confirmed for the selected route.

Documentation and export

For an ashwagandha route, testing and destination documents are confirmed before quoting. Available checks depend on the product and buyer brief; certification or registration is not represented as held without current evidence.
