      Why the result stays simple

      A public tool is useful when it helps someone make a better next decision. It becomes misleading when it turns a few broad inputs into a single profit number or implies that a form submission is an approval. This check therefore shows its assumptions and gaps instead of pretending that a screening result is a bankable plan.

      Good inputs

      Species direction, space, utilities, budget range, timing and a credible route to market.

      Still required for a plan

      Site evidence, technical design, quotations, production assumptions, market validation, compliance and current financing rules.

      Questions people ask

      Does this calculate my mushroom-farm profit?

      No. It is intentionally a readiness screen. A responsible financial model needs site, capacity, yield, price, operating-cost, capex, financing and risk assumptions that cannot be inferred from this short check.

      Why do you show the result before asking for my phone number?

      Because the first value exchange should be useful on its own. If you want a human review after seeing the result, you can choose the advisory route that fits.

      Can a “promising fit” still fail later?

      Yes. The result only means that the answers contain enough early signals for the suggested next conversation. It is not approval, a guarantee or a substitute for validation.
