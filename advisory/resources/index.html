Good Mushroom Advisory resources

Mushroom business questions, explained carefully.

These guides are for founders who want to understand the commercial question behind a mushroom project before they choose a report, a DPR or a custom scope. They are educational starting points, not a substitute for technical, legal, laboratory or regulatory advice.

Use the resource that matches the decision in front of you: whether to proceed, how to structure the facility, what a written DPR should answer, or whether a product is ready for a serious buyer conversation. Each guide states its assumptions and boundaries.

Public reference points

Use primary sources for technical and regulatory questions.

Good Mushroom Advisory can help frame the commercial question, but specialist information should come from the relevant authority or professional. For scientific mushroom-research reference, start with ICAR’s Directorate of Mushroom Research listing. For current food, labelling, packaging and claims material, review the FSSAI regulations hub.
